BitCon Wiring Harness

Diagram

RP2350 (Raspberry Pi Pico 2) rhythm game controller. 9x illuminated button units + 2ch photointerrupter for rotation sensing. All resistors live board-side between the GPIO and the header pin — they appear in the BOM but not in the wiring graph.
